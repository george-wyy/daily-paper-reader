## 摘要
计算系统正从被动工具发展为在用户明确请求之前进行感知、解释、预测和行动的系统。这一转变得益于移动连接的全球规模、可穿戴和环境感知的快速扩展、机器学习和基础模型的进步、分布式边缘基础设施以及物理执行机制。我们将“主动计算”定义为一种范式，其中系统推断用户情境，预测未来需求或风险，并在适当的时间启动信息传递或行动。本综述将主动计算与反应式计算、情境感知计算、自适应计算和预测计算区分开来，并将主动性视为一个跨越感知、理解、决策、行动和治理的系统级集成问题。我们回顾了主动计算的技术推动因素，组织了其设计空间，分析了诸如不确定性感知触发和预测到行动差距等技术挑战，并讨论了涉及用户接受度、信任、隐私、问责、公平性和可持续性的社会技术问题。我们认为，关键的研究挑战不仅在于提高预测准确性，还在于确定系统应何时、如何以及是否代表用户采取行动。

## Abstract
Computing systems are moving from reactive tools toward systems that sense, interpret, predict, and act before explicit user requests. This transition is enabled by the global scale of mobile connectivity, the rapid expansion of wearable and ambient sensing, advances in machine learning and foundation models, distributed edge infrastructure, and physical actuation. We define \emph{proactive computing} as a paradigm in which systems infer user context, anticipate future needs or risks, and initiate information delivery or actions at an appropriate time. This survey distinguishes proactive computing from reactive, context-aware, adaptive, and predictive computing, and frames proactivity as a system-level integration problem across sensing, understanding, decision making, action, and governance. We review the technological enablers of proactive computing, organize its design space, analyze technical challenges such as uncertainty-aware triggering and the prediction-to-action gap, and discuss socio-technical issues involving user acceptance, trust, privacy, accountability, fairness, and sustainability. We argue that the key research challenge is not merely improving prediction accuracy, but determining when, how, and whether systems should act on behalf of users.
