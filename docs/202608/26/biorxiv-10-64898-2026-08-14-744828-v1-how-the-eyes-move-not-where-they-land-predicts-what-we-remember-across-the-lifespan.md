## 摘要
我们在自然场景中移动眼睛的位置和方式取决于场景和观看者两者。观看的空间和时间组织如何随生命周期变化，以及这些变化是否与记忆相关，仍不清楚。我们记录了一个生命周期队列（N = 179，年龄5-79岁）在自由观看自然场景时的眼动，然后测试了在不同图像退化程度下的识别能力。通过每个观察者的观看与同龄人、年轻人以及刺激驱动的显著性模型的接近程度来刻画他们，我们发现了一种发展性分离：眼睛指向位置的一致性随年龄单调增加，而眼睛移动方式（扫视方向、长度和注视持续时间）的一致性呈倒U形，在成年早期达到顶峰。识别敏感性遵循相同形式的倒U形。在所有三个参考框架中，眼睛移动方式的典型性（而非空间定向的典型性）预测了识别能力。

## Abstract
Where and how we move our eyes through a natural scene depends jointly on the scene and on the viewer. How the spatial and temporal organization of viewing changes across the lifespan, and whether those changes relate to memory, remains unclear. We recorded eye movements from a lifespan cohort (N = 179, ages 5-79) during free viewing of naturalistic scenes, then tested recognition across graded levels of image degradation. Characterizing each observer by how closely their viewing corresponded to that of age peers, young adults, and a stimulus-driven salience model, we found a developmental dissociation: consistency in where the eyes were directed increased monotonically with age, whereas consistency in how they moved: saccade direction, length, and fixation duration, followed an inverted-U peaking in young adulthood. Recognition sensitivity followed an inverted-U of the same form. Across all three reference frames, typicality in how the eyes moved, but not in their spatial targeting, predicted recognition.
